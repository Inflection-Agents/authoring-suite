Use the `arch-docs` skill and load `references/05-diagram-list.md`.

Group the diagrams into layers that peel from high level to deep detail. One paragraph each: number and title,
visual type and C4 level, what is on it, the one thing to notice, and why it exists. Then read your own titles back
as the argument the set makes. Draft wide. Wait for approval.
