# Voice Sheet

Elicited 2026-09-12 per `skills/muse-white-paper/references/elicitation.md`.
Three registers (opinion, explainer, work story), 12 A/B pairs, unanimous.
Each rejected line is the liked line with exactly that habit removed.

## Entry 1

- `habit`: chain claims with an explicit consequence marker (which means,
  that means that, meaning that, so). Never bare juxtaposition.
- `liked`: With AI we produce code faster, which means more PRs waiting
  for review.
- `rejected`: With AI we produce code faster. More PRs waiting for review.
- `scope`: cross-register; opinion, explainer, work story. Marker family
  counted 4, 3, 1 across the three transcripts.

## Entry 2

- `habit`: open with the flat verdict before any setup. No arrival narrative.
- `liked`: Code review is the wrong place for humans to sit in this process.
- `rejected`: There are many places humans could sit in this process, and
  after long experience I have come to the view that code review is the
  wrong one.
- `scope`: cross-register; all three transcripts open on a verdict.

## Entry 3

- `habit`: pin terms in parentheses mid-sentence, not in apposition.
- `liked`: Most teams struggled with pull requests (code review) long
  before AI arrived.
- `rejected`: Most teams struggled with pull requests, that is, code
  review, long before AI arrived.
- `scope`: cross-register; (code review), (xhigh)/(low), (SDD).

## Entry 4

- `habit`: attach a quantity or named artifact to every abstraction.
- `liked`: Six developers worked a year with no product and no confidence
  in what they were building.
- `rejected`: A full team worked a long time with no product and no
  confidence in what they were building.
- `scope`: cross-register; days of PR wait, named phases with levels,
  6 developers versus 3 months with 5 fewer engineers.
