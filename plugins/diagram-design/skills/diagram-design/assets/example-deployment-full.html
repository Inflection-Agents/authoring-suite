THE BUDGET QUESTION

Nesting depth is free, content isn't

Region, zone, and cluster are dashed transparent boundaries — they cost nothing against the node budget the way a real instance or infrastructure box does. The 9-node guideline applies to what's inside them, not to how deep they nest.

Three kinds, three shapes

  • Deployment node: dashed, transparent, no fill
  • Container instance: solid, plain corners
  • Infrastructure: solid, notched corners — fill alone was too weak a signal

Asymmetric zones stay separate

Zone A and B could collapse into one template with a "×2 zones" badge if they were identical — but the primary lives in A and the replica in B, so collapsing them would erase the one relationship the diagram exists to show.
