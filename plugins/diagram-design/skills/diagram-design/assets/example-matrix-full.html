Tenant deployment · region × product

Three regions, three products, nine cells — and one claim: Fission is the only product deployed in every region. The whole column carries that claim as a single marked element, not three separate accents on three separate cells.

NO DRAWN GRID

Fills carry the structure

Alternating row tints and one marked column do the entire job a ruled table would — no stroke lines separate a single cell from its neighbor anywhere in this diagram.

Instances, not a count

  • Each tenant is one small chip, not a number
  • A cell with 2 tenants shows 2 chips, not "×2"
  • Chips only — this scenario never needed a "too many to draw" threshold

Empty means empty, on purpose

An em-dash, not a blank cell that could read as an oversight. EU/Beacon and APAC/Certainty are exactly as deliberate as every filled cell — the matrix says so, rather than leaving a reader to guess.
