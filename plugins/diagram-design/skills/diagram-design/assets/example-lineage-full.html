Upstream (what dim_customer derives from) and downstream (what breaks if it changes) get different colours, not just different arrowheads. mart_revenue sits two hops out even though dim_customer also feeds it directly — the layering respects its longest incoming path, not just the nearest one. DIRECTION WITHOUT ARROWHEADS ALONE

Colour carries the axis

Ink for upstream, link-blue for downstream — a reader scanning quickly sees which side is cause and which is consequence before reading a single arrowhead.

Depth is columns, not a number

  • Each hop is its own column, labeled
  • mart_revenue's true depth is 2, from its longest path
  • A skip edge (dim_customer → mart_revenue) is drawn as a real edge, not hidden

The fan-out is honest

A real lineage graph can have one asset feeding thirty dashboards. Collapsing them into a labeled cohort keeps the diagram readable without pretending there are only one or two consumers.
